# Immersive redesign notes

- Home, About, Research and Publications use the fixed Solar System background.
- Research Interests keeps the background and adds a sticky image panel that changes with the selected/visible topic.
- Travel uses `assets/images/pic07.jpg` as a nature-focused fixed background.
- Shared visual overrides live in `assets/css/redesign.css`.
- To change the travel background, edit the `body.site-nature::before` rule in that file.
- Topic artwork and labels are configured in `interests.html` through `data-image`, `data-title`, and `data-caption` attributes.
